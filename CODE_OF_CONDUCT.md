# Code of Conduct

Mita follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

To report a Code of Conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub's private reporting channel:

1. Go to <https://github.com/kleenpulse/kilomita/security>.
2. Click **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Start the title with **"Code of Conduct"** and describe what happened.

Reports are reviewed only by the maintainers and handled confidentially. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
